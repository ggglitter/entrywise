# Demonstration provenance

The stills are actual browser captures of the running Entrywise app, made through
normal UI controls. Receipt URLs point to example.org and are labelled DEMO
EXAMPLE. They do not represent real competition participation.

Version 0.2 replaces the original still-image walkthrough with an edited live
browser demonstration. The 86-second video uses 326 actual frames, captured
through the normal browser controls at about four frames per second. Scene
timings and selected unaltered captures are in capture-provenance.json and
stills-v02/. The raw capture sequence remains in the local evidence workspace.
The current player and MP4 are in docs/demo.html and docs/media/entrywise-demo.mp4.
No music, cloned voice, invented personal reflection or third-party video is used.
The desktop and narrow states and representative encoded frames were inspected.
The older stills are historical QA evidence, not screenshots of every new feature.

Devpost requires a public YouTube or Vimeo link for submission. A repository copy
of the MP4 is a preparation artifact, not evidence that this upload happened.
