# Product requirements

## Core journey
Adjust creator conditions, filter opportunities, inspect checks and sources,
save a plan, and record an independently obtained registration or submission.

## Decisions and assumptions
Free and remote constraints are the participant's.
Working title and interface are agent proposals under the request to make works.
No personal submission copy or learning experience has been attributed to them.

## Look and feel
Editorial desk: paper, dark green ink, orange attention, serif title, aligned
opportunity rows. Desktop queue and evidence sheet; mobile stacking.

## Checkable behavior
- Unknown requirements do not become a pass.
- Student-only and AI-prohibited conditions can exclude a match.
- Registration deadlines are evaluated separately from final delivery.
- Search and decision filters compose with profile changes.
- Saved means planned, not registered.
- Receipts need an HTTP(S) URL and an explicit confirmation.
- Registration and submission remain separate after reload.
- Storage errors are visible.
- Demo examples use a separate namespace and are labelled.

## Boundaries
The app does not verify organizer eligibility or receipt authenticity.
No results has a reset action. Missing sources or exact times require review.
